## Ideas

> ([[2015 - Burgers - Towards Quantum Teleportation from a Spontaneous Parametric Down-Conversion Source to a Quantum Dot Spin by Hong-Ou-Mandel Interference.pdf#page=26&selection=53,0,57,27&color=yellow|p.26]])
> The main topic of this work will be on the optical study and manipulation of QDs. In particular we will focus on optical spin manipulations of a single electron confined to a QD structure. 

The [[Quantum Dots]] (InAs on GaAs) qubit is the spin state of the single electron confined within the QD [[2015 - Burgers - Towards Quantum Teleportation from a Spontaneous Parametric Down-Conversion Source to a Quantum Dot Spin by Hong-Ou-Mandel Interference.pdf#page=27&annotation=1632R|p.27]]. The single qubit gate operation is achieved using an optical pulse of around 2ps. The general coherence time of the electron in us order [[2015 - Burgers - Towards Quantum Teleportation from a Spontaneous Parametric Down-Conversion Source to a Quantum Dot Spin by Hong-Ou-Mandel Interference.pdf#page=28&annotation=1635R|p.28]].

The QDs are embedded in diode structure so that the bias voltage can be applied to lower the lowest conduction level below the Fermi level [[2015 - Burgers - Towards Quantum Teleportation from a Spontaneous Parametric Down-Conversion Source to a Quantum Dot Spin by Hong-Ou-Mandel Interference.pdf#page=35&annotation=1679R|p.35]], so that an election is favored to tunnel into the QD. This makes a charged $X^-$ state (ground state) [[2015 - Burgers - Towards Quantum Teleportation from a Spontaneous Parametric Down-Conversion Source to a Quantum Dot Spin by Hong-Ou-Mandel Interference.pdf#page=36&annotation=1690R|p.36]].
