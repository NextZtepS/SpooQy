## Key Findings

The nonlinear optical coefficient $d_{ijk}(\lambda)$ varies frequency as well according to the Miller's rule with is governed by the Miller indices $\delta_{ijk}$. The Miller indices are approximately independent of frequency giving the approximate dispersion scheme [[1992 - Biaggio - Refractive indices of orthorhombic KNbO3 II Phase-matching configurations for nonlinear-optical interactions.pdf#page=2&selection=358,0,407,33&color=note|p.2]].

### Second Harmonic Generation

[[Potassium Niobate (KNO)|KNO]] is know to have a large temperature dependence, especially $n_c$, which allows it to cover wide range of phase matching (degenerate) wavelengths [[1992 - Biaggio - Refractive indices of orthorhombic KNbO3 II Phase-matching configurations for nonlinear-optical interactions.pdf#page=5&selection=77,10,105,6&color=yellow|p.5]]. 

The homogeneous heating rate is limited at 80 C/h and cooling rate is 40 C/h. As the temperature goes over 180 C, a smaller heating and cooling rates are advisable as the phase transition happpens at 223 C [[1992 - Biaggio - Refractive indices of orthorhombic KNbO3 II Phase-matching configurations for nonlinear-optical interactions.pdf#page=5&selection=119,0,169,2&color=yellow|p.5]].

There are 2 cases of [[NCPM]] possible: along **a** ($P_b + P_b \rightarrow P_c$) and along **b** ($P_a + P_a \rightarrow P_c$) [[1992 - Biaggio - Refractive indices of orthorhombic KNbO3 II Phase-matching configurations for nonlinear-optical interactions.pdf#page=5&selection=170,0,223,50&color=yellow|p.5]].

The non-uniform heating and lack of precise temperature measurment leads to some discrepancy between experiement and prediction [[1992 - Biaggio - Refractive indices of orthorhombic KNbO3 II Phase-matching configurations for nonlinear-optical interactions.pdf#page=5&selection=272,0,290,1&color=red|p.5]].

The bandwidth of the wavlength, angular acceptance, and temperature could be calculated analytically [[1992 - Biaggio - Refractive indices of orthorhombic KNbO3 II Phase-matching configurations for nonlinear-optical interactions.pdf#page=6&selection=384,0,445,11&color=yellow|p.6]] [[1992 - Biaggio - Refractive indices of orthorhombic KNbO3 II Phase-matching configurations for nonlinear-optical interactions.pdf#page=7&selection=5,0,10,12&color=yellow|p.7]].

### Sum-Frequency Generation

![[1992 - Biaggio - Refractive indices of orthorhombic KNbO3 II Phase-matching configurations for nonlinear-optical interactions.pdf#page=7&rect=280,50,537,321&color=yellow|p.7]]


The effective nonlinear coefficient $d_{eff}$ of [[Potassium Niobate (KNO)|KNO]] is much stronger in type-I compared to type-II because $d_{eff}$  vanishes for type-II [[1992 - Biaggio - Refractive indices of orthorhombic KNbO3 II Phase-matching configurations for nonlinear-optical interactions.pdf#page=7&selection=393,0,398,36&color=red|p.7]] [[1992 - Biaggio - Refractive indices of orthorhombic KNbO3 II Phase-matching configurations for nonlinear-optical interactions.pdf#page=10&selection=20,0,44,1&color=red|p.10]].

There are 2 cases of [[NCPM]] possible: along **a** ($P_b + P_b \rightarrow P_c$) and along **b** ($P_a + P_a \rightarrow P_c$) similar to SHG case [[1992 - Biaggio - Refractive indices of orthorhombic KNbO3 II Phase-matching configurations for nonlinear-optical interactions.pdf#page=7&selection=399,0,442,9&color=yellow|p.7]]. 

The phase matching condition for [[NCMP]] could be tuned via temperature for both cases [[1992 - Biaggio - Refractive indices of orthorhombic KNbO3 II Phase-matching configurations for nonlinear-optical interactions.pdf#page=9&selection=262,0,277,31&color=yellow|p.9]].
