## Arguments

Different quantum devices have different requirements (coherence time, coupling efficiency, etc.). A single photon sources that produce indistinguishable photons are necessary to interface them [[2011 - Polyakov et al. - Coalescence of Single Photons Emitted by Disparate Single-Photon Sources The Example of InAs Quantum Dots and Parametric Down-Conversion Sources.pdf#page=1&selection=85,0,96,55&color=important|p.1]].

Different physical single photon sources produce distinguishable photon in general [[2011 - Polyakov et al. - Coalescence of Single Photons Emitted by Disparate Single-Photon Sources The Example of InAs Quantum Dots and Parametric Down-Conversion Sources.pdf#page=1&selection=97,0,98,60&color=red|p.1]].

[[Quantum Dots]] is a good platform for qubit processing because they integrate well with semiconductor system [[2011 - Polyakov et al. - Coalescence of Single Photons Emitted by Disparate Single-Photon Sources The Example of InAs Quantum Dots and Parametric Down-Conversion Sources.pdf#page=1&selection=113,34,116,42&color=important|p.1]].

[[SPDC]] is a good choice for quantum interconnects due to low coupling to environment [[2011 - Polyakov et al. - Coalescence of Single Photons Emitted by Disparate Single-Photon Sources The Example of InAs Quantum Dots and Parametric Down-Conversion Sources.pdf#page=1&selection=116,43,127,40&color=important|p.1]].

Photons from [[Quantum Dots]] and [[SPDC]] are spectrally and temporally very different [[2011 - Polyakov et al. - Coalescence of Single Photons Emitted by Disparate Single-Photon Sources The Example of InAs Quantum Dots and Parametric Down-Conversion Sources.pdf#page=1&selection=133,8,136,16&color=red|p.1]].

The same analysis method from [[2010 Flagg et al.]] is used.

## Results

![[2011 - Polyakov et al. - Coalescence of Single Photons Emitted by Disparate Single-Photon Sources The Example of InAs Quantum Dots and Parametric Down-Conversion Sources.pdf#page=2&rect=48,387,308,749&color=yellow|p.2]]

The [[SPDC]] photons have a spectral linewidth of ~500 GHz compared to 1 GHz of [[Quantum Dots]]; therefore, an **==active tunable spectral filters ([[Fabry-Perot Cavity]])==** on both signal and ilder are used [[2011 - Polyakov et al. - Coalescence of Single Photons Emitted by Disparate Single-Photon Sources The Example of InAs Quantum Dots and Parametric Down-Conversion Sources.pdf#page=1&selection=148,19,167,44&color=yellow|p.1]].

> ([[2011 - Polyakov et al. - Coalescence of Single Photons Emitted by Disparate Single-Photon Sources The Example of InAs Quantum Dots and Parametric Down-Conversion Sources.pdf#page=2&selection=27,25,72,45&color=yellow|p.2]])
>  The spectral character of the QD is shown in Fig. 1(d). It has a linewidth of 1.1 GHz, implying a coherence time of T2 ¼ 0:29 ns. Timedependent fluorescence of the QD is shown in Fig. 1(e), which when fit by an exponential decay and a finite detector time resolution yields a lifetime of T1 ¼ 0:83 ns. Since T2 < 2T1, the coherence time is not lifetime limited.
>  
This means that the system is sensitive to environmental fluctuation rather than energy loss aka. losing phase information (dephasing) faster then energy decay. The lifetime is much longer than the coherence time. ($T_{coherence} = 2T_{lifetime}$ is the ideal case)

The observable inferference is limited by the resolution of the detectors coming close to the cohrence/[[QD Dephasing]] time $T_2$ [[2011 - Polyakov et al. - Coalescence of Single Photons Emitted by Disparate Single-Photon Sources The Example of InAs Quantum Dots and Parametric Down-Conversion Sources.pdf#page=3&selection=252,2,285,39&color=red|p.3]].

Post-selection for the short time delay to remain within the coherence time is used to achieve a higher coalescence probability (similar to HOM visibility) [[2011 - Polyakov et al. - Coalescence of Single Photons Emitted by Disparate Single-Photon Sources The Example of InAs Quantum Dots and Parametric Down-Conversion Sources.pdf#page=4&selection=0,0,98,1&color=yellow|p.4]].

The reduction is coalescence (visibility) is due to the decoherence of [[Quantum Dots]].
