>  SPDC photons are typically shaped after they are generated using programmable filters or spatial light modulators [32,33]. These techniques cause some loss of the downconverted photons, break apart photon pairs, and reduce the SPDC pair generation efficiency. In contrast for BW SPDC, spectral shaping is applied to the pump and then transferred to the downconverted single photons through the process of frequency translation [7,34]. Losses due to spectral shaping of the pump beam can be compensated by increasing the pump power without degrading the SPDC pair generation efficiency.

The cavity and aperiodic-domain-engineering is set during fabrication and does not offer tunability [[2023 - Kuo et al. - Photon-pair production and frequency translation using backward-wave spontaneous parametric downconversion.pdf#page=2&selection=25,38,30,24&color=red|p.44]].

The idler frequency is less sensitive to pump frequency (smaller bandwitdh) compared to that of the signal. Thus, we can use the pump spectral bandwidth to shape the signal's bandwidth [[2023 - Kuo et al. - Photon-pair production and frequency translation using backward-wave spontaneous parametric downconversion.pdf#page=2&selection=205,33,236,47&color=important|p.44]].
