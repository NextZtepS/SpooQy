## Notes

Topological means that the property of the object depends on the global property rather a specific parameter. In this paper, it refers to the *edge states* [[2019 - Mittal - A topological source of quantum light.pdf#page=1&selection=127,6,128,62&color=yellow|p.1]].

A theory behind the edge states can be found in the references included [[2019 - Mittal - A topological source of quantum light.pdf#page=1&selection=155,53,159,22&color=note|p.1]].

The signal and idler are produced along the CW and CCW edge of the 2D lattice of ring resonators which have tractable phase relationship from directional position hopping [[2019 - Mittal - A topological source of quantum light.pdf#page=2&annotation=684R|p.2]].
