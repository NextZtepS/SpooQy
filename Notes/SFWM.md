> > SFWM is a third-order nonlinear process in which two pump photons at frequency $ω_p$ are annihilated and two daughter photons, called signal and idler, are generated at frequencies $ω_s$ and $ω_i$. The spectrum of the generated signal and idler photons as well as their correlations are dictated by energy and momentum conservation relations, i.e., $2ω_p = ω_s + ω_i$ and $2k_p = k_s + k_i$, where $k$’s are the momenta of the respective fields. 

## Hardware

Coupled ring resonator arrays is a useful device that can select particular spectral mode due to resonance property and increase the efficiency at the same time [[Mittal 2019]] [[2019 - Mittal - A topological source of quantum light.pdf#page=1&selection=111,7,114,29&color=yellow|p.1]], [[Fukuda et al. 2005]] [[2005 - Fukuda et al. - Four-wave mixing in silicon wire waveguides.pdf#page=6&selection=20,37,22,51&color=yellow|p.6]].

The hardware fabrication capabilities often limits the consistency of the photonic properties achieved [[Mittal 2019]] [[2019 - Mittal - A topological source of quantum light.pdf#page=1&selection=114,30,118,51&color=red|p.1]].

*Edge States* achieved by utilizing the 2D ring resonator lattice's topological structure has been shown to improve the consistency in [[Mittal 2019]].
