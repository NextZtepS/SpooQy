
## Monolithic

Monolithic refers to the design that uses the crystal itself as a cavity by coating the end faces. Temperature is used to select different cavity modes [[2026 Barcons Planas]] [[2026 - Barcons Planas - Pure narrowband photon-pair generation in a monolithic cavity.pdf#page=2&selection=27,0,30,41&color=note|p.2]]. 

Microring resonators are a representative example of this approach in waveguides. The main challenge of waveguide-based approach is intra-cavity losses that limit the coupling efficieny reducing the brightness [[2026 Barcons Planas]] [[2026 - Barcons Planas - Pure narrowband photon-pair generation in a monolithic cavity.pdf#page=2&selection=38,25,47,11&color=red|p.2]].
