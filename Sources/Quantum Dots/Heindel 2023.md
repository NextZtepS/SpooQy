## Objective

> [!PDF|important] [[2023 - Heindel - Quantum dots for photonic quantum information technology.pdf#page=6&selection=28,25,43,33&color=important|p.6]]
> > this review article gives a comprehensive overview of the development and various application perspectives of semiconductor QDs in the field of photonic quantum information technology, as illustrated in Fig. 1. The article is aimed at students and scientists who want to get a well-founded insight into the basics of QDs, highly optimized device concepts, modern nanoprocessing technologies and the optical and quantum-optical properties of corresponding QLSs and IQPCs.
