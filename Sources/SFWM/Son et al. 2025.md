## Main motivation

> ([[2025 - Son et al. - Generation of photon pairs through spontaneous four-wave mixing in thin nonlinear layers.pdf#page=1&selection=85,53,89,95|2025 - Son et al. - Generation of photon pairs through spontaneous four-wave mixing in thin nonlinear layers, p.1]])
> Despite the success in the demonstration of spontaneous parametric-down-conversion (SPDC) from such ‘flat’ sources, there are almost no works on spontaneous four-wave mixing (SFWM) – an alternative process to generate photon pairs. Meanwhile, SFWM can be implemented in any nanostructures, including isotropic and centrosymmetric ones, which are easier to fabricate than crystalline structures needed for SPDC

## Notes

### SiN layers

For SFWM in SiN layers, higher Nitrogen concentration reduces $\chi^{(3)}$ aka. brightness while reduces undesirable photoluminescence. So, the paper is trying to look for an optimal concentration [[2025 - Son et al. - Generation of photon pairs through spontaneous four-wave mixing in thin nonlinear layers.pdf#page=2&annotation=251R|p.2]]. In the end, they confirm it [[2025 - Son et al. - Generation of photon pairs through spontaneous four-wave mixing in thin nonlinear layers.pdf#page=3&annotation=262R|p.3]].

Phase matching condition is relaxed (thought of as automatically true) in thin material or low-dispersion material [[2025 - Son et al. - Generation of photon pairs through spontaneous four-wave mixing in thin nonlinear layers.pdf#page=2&annotation=254R|p.2]].

The generated photons are dominated by photoluminescence rather than [[SFWM]] that should has a deterministic polarization [[2025 - Son et al. - Generation of photon pairs through spontaneous four-wave mixing in thin nonlinear layers.pdf#page=3&annotation=270R|p.3]] which is confirmed by cubic pump power dependence [[2025 - Son et al. - Generation of photon pairs through spontaneous four-wave mixing in thin nonlinear layers.pdf#page=3&annotation=273R|p.3]].

### Lithium Niobate

They claim that the signal (700 nm) is due to [[SFWM]] because it quadratic with the pump power which make sense [[2025 - Son et al. - Generation of photon pairs through spontaneous four-wave mixing in thin nonlinear layers.pdf#page=6&selection=61,92,63,51&color=yellow|p.6]].

The idler (1550 nm) depends linearly with pump power which they offer an explanation why this is the case but I do not follow [[2025 - Son et al. - Generation of photon pairs through spontaneous four-wave mixing in thin nonlinear layers.pdf#page=6&selection=64,0,81,88&color=red|p.6]] to [[2025 - Son et al. - Generation of photon pairs through spontaneous four-wave mixing in thin nonlinear layers.pdf#page=7&selection=0,1,2,14&color=red|p.7]].

The pairs generation rate is much higher than the SiN layers as LiNbO3 produces much less photoluminescence [[2025 - Son et al. - Generation of photon pairs through spontaneous four-wave mixing in thin nonlinear layers.pdf#page=7&selection=3,0,12,31&color=yellow|p.7]]

The polarization visibility and the cross correlation is also much higher but still not that good. ![[2025 - Son et al. - Generation of photon pairs through spontaneous four-wave mixing in thin nonlinear layers.pdf#page=6&rect=141,400,471,700&color=yellow|p.6]]
