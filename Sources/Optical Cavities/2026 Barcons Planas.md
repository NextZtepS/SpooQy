## Key Arguments

Photons generated from SPDC are spatio-spectral correlated, meaning its spectrum varies with spatial mode/collection. The correlation comes from the energy and momentum conservations between the generated pairs [[2026 - Barcons Planas - Pure narrowband photon-pair generation in a monolithic cavity.pdf#page=1&selection=100,19,104,80&color=yellow|p.1]].

Placing nonlinear crystal in an optical cavity can enhance the spectral brightness of a resonant compared the non-resonant and yield smaller bandwidths [[2026 - Barcons Planas - Pure narrowband photon-pair generation in a monolithic cavity.pdf#page=2&selection=15,16,18,73&color=yellow|p.2]].

Existing cavities rely on mirrors which are complicated and actively stabilized. This type of cavities need extran filtering on adjacent spectrol modes in the clusters [[2026 - Barcons Planas - Pure narrowband photon-pair generation in a monolithic cavity.pdf#page=2&selection=18,75,26,57&color=red|p.2]].

Monolithic cavity (using crystal itself as a cavity) should offer a simple design where temperature is the only parameter [[2026 - Barcons Planas - Pure narrowband photon-pair generation in a monolithic cavity.pdf#page=2&selection=27,0,30,41&color=important|p.2]].

## Key Results

The cavity-enhanced generated photons are at 1540 nm which is good for C-band telecom. They achieve 70% heralding efficiency, $96 \pm 3$ % main cavity mode spectral purity, $91 \pm 9$ % HOM visibility [[2026 - Barcons Planas - Pure narrowband photon-pair generation in a monolithic cavity.pdf#page=2&selection=50,0,86,1&color=yellow|p.2]]. **The main question is why their uncertainty is so large.**
