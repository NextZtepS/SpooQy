## Notes

The higher beam intensity due to the smaller confinement makes the nonlinear optical effect much stronger in silicon waveguides compared to single-mode fibre [[2005 - Fukuda et al. - Four-wave mixing in silicon wire waveguides.pdf#page=2&selection=98,54,102,52&color=yellow|p.2]].

In this work, [[SFWM]] is not desired as it produces the crosstalk in wavelength-multiplexing applications [[2005 - Fukuda et al. - Four-wave mixing in silicon wire waveguides.pdf#page=2&selection=111,0,112,83&color=red|p.2]].

The pump is at $\nu_1$ and the idler is at  $\nu_2$. They combine both of them in a straight silicon waveguide such that the output contains extra $2\nu_1 - \nu_2$ and $2\nu_2 - \nu_1$ frequencies.

> The detuning is defined as the wavelength difference between pump and idler lights. 

Ring resonators can be used to increase the conversion efficiency as the resonant frequencies exemplifies that power density. However, the **free-spectral range** (the spacing between adjacent resonant frequencies) puts more constraints on the possible frequencies involved [[2005 - Fukuda et al. - Four-wave mixing in silicon wire waveguides.pdf#page=6&selection=20,37,22,51&color=yellow|p.6]].
