## Main Argument

> ([[2015 - Silverstone et al. - Qubit entanglement between ring-resonator photon-pair sources on a silicon chip.pdf#page=1&selection=29,42,31,32&color=important|p.1]])
> We show that ring-resonator-based spontaneous four-wave mixing photon-pair sources can be made highly indistinguishable and that their spectral correlations are small.

## Notes

It is more common for the integrated photon-pair source to use the polarization rather the path to create entanglement [[2015 - Silverstone et al. - Qubit entanglement between ring-resonator photon-pair sources on a silicon chip.pdf#page=2&selection=119,3,121,44&color=yellow|p.2]].

The generation uses 2 pump photons to create a pair of the signal and idler that are 1 [[free spectral range]] (a gap between cavity's adjacent resonance frequencies) on both side of the pump [[2015 - Silverstone et al. - Qubit entanglement between ring-resonator photon-pair sources on a silicon chip.pdf#page=2&selection=347,57,374,1&color=yellow|p.2]].

The efficiency of the process is very low and produces only 30 coincidences per second [[2015 - Silverstone et al. - Qubit entanglement between ring-resonator photon-pair sources on a silicon chip.pdf#page=2&selection=375,0,399,11&color=red|p.2]].

Since the signal and idler are of different wavelength, it is hard to study their interferance directly. They uses demultiplexers (double-bus microring resonators) to analyze the idlers from the 2 sources using MZI instead [[2015 - Silverstone et al. - Qubit entanglement between ring-resonator photon-pair sources on a silicon chip.pdf#page=2&selection=424,24,459,32&color=yellow|p.2]].

The superposition of path information comes from the fact that the pump has only enough energy to produce a photon-pair in one of the ring resonators only [[2015 - Silverstone et al. - Qubit entanglement between ring-resonator photon-pair sources on a silicon chip.pdf#page=4&selection=210,2,232,46&color=yellow|p.4]].

The photon-piar must be relay minimal information about each other's spectral/frequency to produce a high-purity entanglement because the spectral difference erases the indistinguishability of the entagled state. This can be investigated using [[Joint Spectral Density]] [[2015 - Silverstone et al. - Qubit entanglement between ring-resonator photon-pair sources on a silicon chip.pdf#page=4&annotation=347R|p.4]].
