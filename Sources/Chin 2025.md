## Important Arguments

Noncritical phasematching ([[NCPM]]) is simpler and more stable againts temperature change for highly nondegenerate entanglement photon pairs generation [[2025 - Chin - Highly nondegenerate polarization-entangled photon pairs produced through noncritical phasematching in single-domain KTiOPO4.pdf#page=1&selection=30,0,36,34|page 1]].

Ciritical phasematching requires a strict temperature control within 0.1°C [[2025 - Chin - Highly nondegenerate polarization-entangled photon pairs produced through noncritical phasematching in single-domain KTiOPO4.pdf#page=1&selection=85,35,93,52|page 1]].

[[NCPM]] produces photons at around 1080 nm in between the O and C-band and does not offer tunability as it has less varying parameters [[2025 - Chin - Highly nondegenerate polarization-entangled photon pairs produced through noncritical phasematching in single-domain KTiOPO4.pdf#page=1&selection=184,51,193,49|page 1]].

[[NCPM]] is generally not as bright as [[QPM]] for type-0 generation in [[ppKTP crystal]]; however, KNbO3 is expected to have the same brightness for type-0 [[2025 - Chin - Highly nondegenerate polarization-entangled photon pairs produced through noncritical phasematching in single-domain KTiOPO4.pdf#page=2&selection=86,36,95,37|page 2.]]

For [[QPM]], the temperature dependent property of the [[ppKTP crystal]] is accounted by the change in refractive index rather than the change in pooling period [[2025 - Chin - Highly nondegenerate polarization-entangled photon pairs produced through noncritical phasematching in single-domain KTiOPO4.pdf#page=4&selection=87,0,124,21|page 4]].

Small linewidth of output is desirable because it is easier to be distinguished from noise and has less chromatic disperson in optical fiber [[2025 - Chin - Highly nondegenerate polarization-entangled photon pairs produced through noncritical phasematching in single-domain KTiOPO4.pdf#page=4&selection=145,0,156,24|page 4]].
