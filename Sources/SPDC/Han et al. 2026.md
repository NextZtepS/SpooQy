## Key Results

> ([[2026 - Han et al. - Broadband tunable photon-pair generation and spectrum measurement based on noncritical lithium niobate crystals.pdf#page=1&selection=291,33,321,3&color=note|p.1]])
> Type-I SPDC in an x-cut LN crystal pumped by a 532 nm continuous-wave (CW) laser. By operating the crystal under NCPM conditions at temperatures up to 250◦C, the generated photon pairs can be tuned across the NIR spectrum from 800 nm to 1600 nm, while maintaining a high coincidence-to-accidental ratio (CAR > 20 dB). 

> ([[2026 - Han et al. - Broadband tunable photon-pair generation and spectrum measurement based on noncritical lithium niobate crystals.pdf#page=2&selection=133,14,147,11&color=note|p.2]])
> Given that the second-harmonic generation of 1064 nm at 108.9◦C was observed, by controlling the temperature above this point, the phase matching of two new wavelengths will be automatically satisfied towards both sides.
> 
> At the inverse progress of SHG at $108.9$ C, the degenerate pairs of $1064$ nm should be created from $532$ nm pump. At higher temperature, the changes in refractive index should put the signal and idler apart due to different phase matching conditions.

The spectrum width of the atomic CO transition is extremely narrow while the width of the SPDC is 3 order of magnitudes larger, not giving us the detail of absorption lines [[2026 - Han et al. - Broadband tunable photon-pair generation and spectrum measurement based on noncritical lithium niobate crystals.pdf#page=3&selection=85,0,106,24&color=red|p.3]].

Lower temperature range offers higher resolution in wavelength tuning due to less change in refractive index [[2026 - Han et al. - Broadband tunable photon-pair generation and spectrum measurement based on noncritical lithium niobate crystals.pdf#page=3&selection=157,0,177,12&color=yellow|p.3]].

[[NCPM]] in [[LN crystal]] offers wide temperature tunability with temperature [[2026 - Han et al. - Broadband tunable photon-pair generation and spectrum measurement based on noncritical lithium niobate crystals.pdf#page=1&selection=272,0,279,1&color=yellow|p.1]].
