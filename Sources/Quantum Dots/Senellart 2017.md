## Notes

This review focuses on the InGaAs-based [[Quantum Dots]] [[2017 - Senellart - High-performance semiconductor quantum-dot single-photon sources.pdf#page=4&selection=233,2,239,19&color=note|p.1029]].

[[Hanbery Brown and Twiss]] experiment is used to characterize the [[single-photon purity]] of the source [[2017 - Senellart - High-performance semiconductor quantum-dot single-photon sources.pdf#page=2&selection=92,0,98,1&color=note|p.1027]].

[[Hong-Ou-Mandel]] (HOM) experiment offers a quantitative characterization of the **indistinguishability** of the photon wacepackets [[2017 - Senellart - High-performance semiconductor quantum-dot single-photon sources.pdf#page=2&selection=14,0,49,4&color=note|p.1027]].

The speed of quantum communication depends on many factors of the source. Probability that a light pulse contains a single photon $B$ tells us the initial performace of the link before other loss factor and repetition rate [[2017 - Senellart - High-performance semiconductor quantum-dot single-photon sources.pdf#page=3&selection=46,11,63,1&color=note|p.1028]].
